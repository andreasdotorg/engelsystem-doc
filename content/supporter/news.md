# Communicating with Your Team

As a supporter, you'll need to keep your team informed. While the news feature is typically managed by event admins, you have several ways to reach your angels.

## Angel Type Description

Your angel type has a description that all team members see when they view the type. This is the primary place to communicate:

- Requirements for joining
- What to expect during shifts
- Meeting points and times
- Contact information
- Links to external resources

Work with your event's admins to keep this description current. Changes here are visible to everyone browsing angel types, not just your confirmed members.

## During Shifts

The most effective communication happens in person. Use shift handoffs to pass along information, catch up with team members during their work, and be present at your usual location so people know where to find you.

Having a way to reach team members during shifts makes coordination much easier. Include contact information in your angel type description. For urgent matters, you can reach individual angels through the contact info in their profiles.

## Working with Administrators

Administrators can help you reach your team through official channels:

**News posts.** If you need to announce something to all angels (not just your team), talk to an administrator. They can post news items that appear on the main page and get sent as email notifications to angels who've opted in.

**Meetings.** Introduction meetings and team gatherings appear in the Meetings section of the system. Administrators can create these. If you're running an introduction for your angel type, coordinate with them to get it listed.

**Bulk messages.** The system can send messages to specific groups. If you need to reach all confirmed members of your angel type, administrators may be able to help arrange this.

## Angel Type Meetings

Many teams hold introduction meetings early in the event. These are your chance to explain:

- What the work actually involves
- Safety considerations and procedures
- Where to get equipment
- Who the supporters are and how to reach them
- Common problems and how to handle them

After the meeting, you'll likely have a queue of angels to confirm. This is normal - the meeting is what qualifies them to join.

{{% notice tip %}}
Face-to-face communication beats digital every time. Make yourself available at predictable times and places. Your team will learn where to find you when they have questions.
{{% /notice %}}

## External Channels

Some teams use external communication channels like Matrix rooms, IRC channels, or mailing lists. If your team has these:

- Mention them in your angel type description
- Don't assume everyone will join them
- Keep critical information in the Engelsystem too

The Engelsystem should remain the authoritative source for shift signups and team membership. External channels are supplements, not replacements.
