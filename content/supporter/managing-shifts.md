# Managing Shifts

As a supporter, you can help your team members get signed up for shifts. This is particularly useful when coordinating with angels in person or helping fill urgent gaps.

## Signing Up Team Members

When you're a supporter for an angel type, you can sign up any confirmed member of your team for shifts that need that angel type. This works for shifts where:

- The angel type matches one you support
- The team member is confirmed in your angel type
- The shift still has available slots

To sign up a team member:

1. Find the shift on the shift schedule
2. Click to add someone to the shift
3. Select the team member from your list
4. Confirm the signup

The angel will receive a notification about being signed up. Use this capability thoughtfully - the angel needs to actually be available and willing to work that shift.

## When to Use This

Supporter sign-up powers are meant to help, not to bypass normal workflows. Good uses include:

**Coordinating in person.** You're at the venue talking to a team member who wants a shift but can't access a computer right now. You sign them up while they're standing there.

**Filling urgent gaps.** A shift is about to start and you know someone who's available. You can reach them, confirm they're willing, and sign them up.

**Training coordination.** You're organizing who shadows whom on technical shifts. Signing people up together ensures they're paired correctly.

**Onboarding new members.** A newcomer isn't sure how to use the system yet, so you walk them through shifts and sign them up for their first few.

{{% notice warning %}}
Never sign someone up for a shift without their knowledge and consent. That's not helping - it's creating problems for everyone.
{{% /notice %}}

## Removing Team Members from Shifts

Sometimes you need to remove someone from a shift they're signed up for. Maybe they're sick, maybe there was a scheduling conflict, maybe they asked you to remove them because they can't access the system.

You can remove team members from shifts in your angel type when they can't remove themselves (for instance, if the self-signout deadline has passed). Contact a shift coordinator if you need shifts cancelled that are outside your angel type.

## What You Can't Do

Supporter powers are limited to your own angel type:

- You can't sign up angels for shifts requiring other angel types
- You can't create new shifts (that requires admin access)
- You can't modify shift times or requirements

If you see systemic scheduling issues - too few shifts, wrong times, not enough slots - talk to a shift coordinator or the event organizers. Your perspective as a team lead is valuable for planning.

## Working with Shift Coordinators

Shift coordinators manage shifts across all angel types and have broader permissions. If you're struggling to fill shifts or notice patterns in your team's availability, let them know. They might be able to adjust shift schedules, recruit more angels to your type, or find other solutions.

{{% notice tip %}}
Keep shift coordinators informed about your team's capacity. If you're chronically understaffed or overstaffed, that's useful information for planning current and future events.
{{% /notice %}}
