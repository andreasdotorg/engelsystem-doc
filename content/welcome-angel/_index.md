Welcome Angels staff the check-in desk where volunteers arrive and register their presence at the event. Your role is essential for tracking who's on-site and ensuring accurate attendance records.

## What Welcome Angels Do

When volunteers arrive at the event, they come to you. You verify their identity, mark them as arrived in the system, and help them get oriented. This is often a volunteer's first interaction with the event's organization, so you're setting the tone for their experience.

## Required Permissions

To function as a Welcome Angel, you need to be a member of the **Welcome Angel** group. This group grants:

| Privilege | What it does |
|-----------|--------------|
| `admin_arrive` | Mark users as arrived at the event |
| `users.arrive.list` | Access the arrival list page |
| `user.info.hint` | See indicators when a user's personal info is restricted |

You should also be in the **Angel** group for basic system access.

## Getting Started

1. **Access the arrival list** - Navigate to **Admin > Arrived** or use the direct link in your navigation
2. **Search for the volunteer** - Use their name or nickname to find them
3. **Verify their identity** - Check that you're marking the right person
4. **Mark them as arrived** - Click the arrival button next to their name
