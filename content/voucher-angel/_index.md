Voucher Angels distribute vouchers to volunteers who have earned them through their work. These might be food vouchers, drink tokens, or other rewards your event offers.

## What Voucher Angels Do

Your role is to verify eligibility and record voucher distribution. Volunteers come to you after working shifts, and you check whether they've earned vouchers and mark them as distributed in the system.

## How Vouchers Work

The voucher system varies by event configuration. Volunteers typically earn vouchers based on hours worked, though some events give vouchers upon arrival. Coordinators can also manually award vouchers when needed. The exact number earned depends on your event's settings—hours required per voucher, maximum vouchers allowed, and so on.

## Getting Started

Navigate to the arrival/user list to find the volunteer by name or nickname. Check their current voucher count and eligibility, then update the voucher count when you hand out vouchers.

{{% notice tip %}}
Keep track of your physical voucher stock. The system tracks what's been recorded as distributed, but you need to ensure you have vouchers to hand out.
{{% /notice %}}
