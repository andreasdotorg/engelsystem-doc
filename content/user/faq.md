Engelsystem provides two ways to get answers: the FAQ for common questions and the Q&A system for specific inquiries.

## Using the FAQ

Navigate to **FAQ** in the main menu to browse frequently asked questions.

FAQ entries are organized by category and contain answers to common questions about:
- The event
- Volunteer procedures
- Shift rules
- Rewards and goodies
- Technical help

Search or browse categories to find what you need.

## Asking Questions

If the FAQ doesn't have your answer, you can submit a question directly to the organizers.

### How to Ask a Question

1. Navigate to **Questions** in the menu
2. Click **Ask a question**
3. Write your question clearly
4. Submit

### What Happens Next

- Your question goes into a queue for coordinators
- A Shift Coordinator or similar role will answer
- You'll see the answer in your Questions page
- You may also receive a notification

### Tips for Good Questions

- **Be specific** - "Where do I pick up my t-shirt?" is better than "t-shirt?"
- **Check the FAQ first** - Your question might already be answered
- **Include context** - If it's about a specific shift or location, mention it

{{% notice note %}}
Questions are not public. Only you and coordinators can see your questions and answers.
{{% /notice %}}
