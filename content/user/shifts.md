# Working with Shifts

Shifts are the core of the Engelsystem. This page explains how to find, sign up for, and manage your shifts.

## Finding Shifts

The shift overview shows all available shifts. You can filter by:

- **Date/Time** - Find shifts that fit your schedule
- **Location** - Work in areas you're familiar with
- **Angel Type** - Filter by the type of work

Each shift listing shows:

- Start and end time
- Location name
- Required angel type
- Number of positions (filled/total)
- Description of the work

## Signing Up

To sign up for a shift:

1. Find a shift with available positions
2. Make sure you meet any requirements (angel type membership)
3. Click to sign up
4. The shift appears in your personal schedule

{{% notice note %}}
Some shifts require you to be a member of a specific angel type. Check the angel type requirements if you can't sign up for a shift you're interested in.
{{% /notice %}}

## Shift Requirements

Shifts may have different access levels based on their angel type:

### Self-Signup Shifts
Anyone who is a member of the angel type can sign up for these shifts directly. No approval needed.

### Restricted Shifts
Require membership in a specific angel type that has restricted access:
- You may need supporter approval to join the angel type first
- Check the angel type description for requirements

## Signing Out

If you can't make a shift:

1. Sign out as early as possible
2. There may be a deadline before the shift starts (configurable by administrators)
3. After the deadline, contact a shift coordinator for help

{{% notice warning %}}
Leaving a shift unfilled affects the whole event. Always sign out properly if you can't attend.
{{% /notice %}}

## Your Shift Schedule

Your personal shift schedule shows all shifts you've signed up for. You can:

- View your upcoming shifts
- Export your schedule to a calendar via iCal
- See your past shifts and accumulated hours

## Night Shifts

Some events configure bonus multipliers for shifts during certain hours (typically overnight). These shifts count extra toward your total hours, encouraging coverage during less popular times.

## Shift Details

Each shift page shows:

- **Time and Location** - When and where to work
- **Description** - What the work involves
- **Signed-up Angels** - Who else is on this shift
- **Needed Angels** - How many positions remain open
