Your profile contains your personal information, settings, and certifications. Keep it updated to ensure smooth coordination at the event.

## Accessing Your Profile

Click your name in the navigation or go to **Settings** to access your profile.

## Personal Information

### Basic Info

- **Nickname** - Your display name throughout the system
- **Name** - Your real name (may be required for some events)
- **Email** - Used for notifications and password recovery
- **Mobile** - Contact number for urgent coordination

### Pronoun/Form of Address

Some events let you set how you'd like to be addressed. This helps coordinators and fellow volunteers communicate respectfully.

### DECT

At events with internal phone systems (like the CCC events), you can register your DECT phone number for easy contact.

## Settings

### Language

Choose your preferred language for the interface. Changes take effect immediately.

### Theme

Select a visual theme if your event offers multiple options.

### News/Email Notifications

Configure how you want to be notified about news and updates.

## Certifications & Qualifications

Depending on your event, you may need to record certain qualifications:

### IFSG Certificate

The German Infection Protection Act (Infektionsschutzgesetz) requires food handlers to have certification. If you'll work with food, you may need to confirm your IFSG status.

### Driver License

Some shifts require drivers. Record your license status and vehicle availability if you can drive for the event.

### First Aid

Events may track first aid certification for safety-relevant shifts.

### Free-From / Dietary

Record dietary restrictions or allergies. This helps with food distribution and meal planning.

{{% notice note %}}
Some certification fields may be restricted to coordinators. If you can't edit something yourself, ask a Shift Coordinator to update it.
{{% /notice %}}

## T-Shirt Size

Set your preferred t-shirt size for goodie distribution. Check this before the event to ensure you receive the right size.

## API Key

Your profile includes an API key for programmatic access. This is useful for:
- Calendar integration with external apps
- Custom tools and integrations
- Automation scripts

Keep your API key secret - it provides access to your account.

## Changing Your Password

1. Go to Settings
2. Find the password section
3. Enter your current password and new password
4. Save changes

## Privacy Note

Some of your information may be visible to other volunteers or coordinators, depending on event configuration. Fields marked as restricted are only visible to users with appropriate permissions.
