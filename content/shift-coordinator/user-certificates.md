Shift Coordinators can view and manage volunteer certifications and qualifications that affect shift eligibility.

## IFSG Certificate

The German Infection Protection Act (Infektionsschutzgesetz) requires certification for food handling.

### What it means
- Volunteers with IFSG certification can work food-related shifts
- The certificate confirms they've been briefed on food hygiene requirements

### Managing IFSG status
1. Navigate to the user's profile
2. Find the IFSG section
3. Update their certification status
4. Save

## Driver License

Track who can drive for the event.

### Driver information
- License status (has license or not)
- Vehicle availability
- Vehicle type (car, van, truck)

### Why it matters
- Some shifts require drivers
- Useful for logistics and transport planning
- Helps with emergency response coordination

## First Aid

Track first aid certification for safety-relevant positions.

{{% notice note %}}
First aid certification editing requires Bureaucrat privileges (`user.fa.edit`). Shift Coordinators can view but not edit.
{{% /notice %}}

## Free-From / Dietary

Track dietary restrictions and allergies for meal planning and food distribution.

### Common uses
- Ensuring appropriate food options at meals
- Avoiding allergens in food preparation areas
- Planning catering quantities

## Viewing Certificate Status

To check a volunteer's certifications:

1. Navigate to their profile
2. View the certifications section
3. Check each relevant certification status

## Bulk Updates

For events with many volunteers needing certification updates:
- Use the user administration interface for batch operations
- Consider having dedicated briefing sessions where certifications can be confirmed
