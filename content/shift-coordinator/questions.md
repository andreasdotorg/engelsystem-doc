Volunteers can submit questions through the Q&A system. Shift Coordinators review and answer these questions.

## Required Privilege

You need the `question.edit` privilege to view and answer questions. This is included in the Shift Coordinator group.

## Accessing Questions

Navigate to **Questions** in the admin menu to see all submitted questions.

The queue shows:
- Who asked the question
- When it was submitted
- The question text
- Current status (answered/unanswered)

## Answering Questions

To answer a question:

1. Click the question to open it
2. Read the full question
3. Write your answer
4. Submit the answer

The volunteer will see your answer on their Questions page.

## Writing Good Answers

- **Be direct** - Answer the actual question
- **Be specific** - Include details like locations, times, who to contact
- **Be friendly** - Remember these are volunteers helping your event
- **Link to resources** - Point to FAQ or documentation if helpful

## Managing Question Volume

During events, questions can pile up. Strategies for managing:

- **Check regularly** - Set times to review the queue
- **Divide by topic** - Have different coordinators handle different question types
- **Create FAQ entries** - Repeated questions should become FAQ entries
- **Clear communication** - Good announcements reduce questions

## Identifying Patterns

Watch for patterns in questions:

- **Many questions about the same topic** - Add an FAQ entry or news post
- **Confusion about a process** - Clarify documentation or procedures
- **Missing information** - Update relevant pages

{{% notice tip %}}
If you see the same question repeatedly, it's time to post a news announcement or add an FAQ entry rather than answering individually.
{{% /notice %}}
