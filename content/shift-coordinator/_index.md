# Shift Coordinator Guide

Shift Coordinators manage shifts across the entire event. While supporters focus on their own angel type's team members, shift coordinators work with the shift schedule itself - creating, editing, and organizing the work that needs to be done.

## What Shift Coordinators Do

Your primary responsibility is making sure shifts exist for angels to sign up for. This means creating shifts that match the event's needs, ensuring each shift has the right angel type requirements, and adjusting the schedule as circumstances change.

The role requires understanding both the technical side (how shifts work in the system) and the operational side (what the event actually needs). You'll often work closely with location managers and team leads to understand staffing requirements.

## Shift Coordinator Capabilities

As a shift coordinator, you can:

- **Create shifts** manually or in bulk for specific time periods
- **Edit shift details** including times, locations, and descriptions
- **Set angel type requirements** - how many of each type a shift needs
- **Delete shifts** when they're no longer needed
- **Import schedules** from external systems like Frab or Pretalx

What you typically can't do (these require supporter status for specific types):

- Confirm or deny angel type applications
- Sign up angels for shifts outside your own types
- Manage team membership

## Understanding Shift Structure

Shifts in Engelsystem have several components:

**Shift Types** categorize the kind of work. A shift type like "Info Desk" or "Security Patrol" defines what the work involves. Multiple shifts can share the same type.

**Locations** are where shifts happen - rooms, entrances, stages. Each location can host different kinds of shifts at different times.

**Needed Angel Types** define who can work a shift. A shift might need two general helpers and one bar-trained angel, for example.

**Time Slots** are when the shift runs. Night shifts (typically 2:00-6:00) often have hour multipliers to compensate for unsociable hours.

## Getting Started

When you first log in with shift coordinator privileges, familiarize yourself with:

1. The existing shift types and what they represent
2. The locations where shifts can happen
3. Any imported schedules from external event systems
4. The angel types available and their restrictions

The Admin menu contains the shift management tools you'll use most.
