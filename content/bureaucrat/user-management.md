Managing user accounts is a core part of bureaucrat work. This page covers how to find users, view their information, and make common updates.

## Finding Users

The user administration area lets you search for angels by name, nickname, or email. You can also filter by various criteria like arrival status or angel type membership. For large events, effective searching saves significant time.

When you find a user, you'll see their profile with personal information, contact details, angel type memberships, shift history, and work log entries.

## Common Tasks

### Marking Arrival

When angels check in at the venue, someone needs to mark them as arrived in the system. This is often done at a registration desk or event entrance. Only arrived angels can sign up for shifts (depending on event configuration).

Look for the arrival status on the user's profile. Marking someone as arrived unlocks their ability to take shifts.

### Angel Type Management

You can confirm angels for restricted angel types across all teams. This is useful when:

- A supporter isn't available to confirm their team members
- Someone has certification documents that need verification
- Bulk processing is needed after an introduction meeting

You can also remove angels from angel types if circumstances require it.

### Work Log Entries

Sometimes shift hours need manual adjustment. Work logs let you add, edit, or remove hour entries for a user. Common reasons:

- An angel worked but wasn't properly signed up for the shift
- Hours need correction due to a data entry error
- Credit for work done outside the normal shift system

Document why you're making changes. The system logs who made modifications and when.

### Goodie Status

Most events give angels something in exchange for their work - typically t-shirts. The goodie system tracks who's eligible and who's already received theirs.

You can mark when someone has picked up their goodie, adjust their eligibility, or handle edge cases like exchanges or additional items.

### Vouchers

Some events use voucher systems for food, drinks, or other items. If enabled, you can view and edit an angel's voucher balance.

## User Profiles

When viewing a user's profile, you'll see several sections:

**Personal Information.** Name, nickname, pronouns, contact details. Some fields may be editable depending on your permissions.

**Angel Types.** Which types they're a member of, their status in each, and whether they're a supporter.

**Shifts.** Their upcoming and past shifts, with times and locations.

**Work Log.** A record of all hours credited to them, whether from shifts or manual entries.

**Settings.** Their preferences for notifications, language, and other options.

## Privacy Considerations

User data should be treated carefully. Don't share personal information outside what's needed for event operations. If someone asks you to look up another angel's details, consider whether there's a legitimate operational reason for the request.

{{% notice note %}}
The system logs administrative actions. If you're making unusual changes to user accounts, be prepared to explain why if asked later.
{{% /notice %}}

## Handling Problems

Sometimes you'll encounter account issues:

**Duplicate accounts.** If someone created multiple accounts, work with your event's admins to merge or deactivate the extras.

**Incorrect information.** Help angels correct their profiles. For sensitive changes, you might ask them to verify their identity.

**Access issues.** If someone can't log in, check if their account exists and whether there are any blocks or restrictions. Password resets may require admin access.

When in doubt, escalate to someone with higher permissions rather than guessing.
