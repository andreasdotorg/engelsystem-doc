While major event setup is typically handled by admins before the event begins, bureaucrats often help with operational tasks during the event. This page covers what you might encounter.

## Understanding the Event Structure

The Engelsystem organizes an event around several key elements:

**Locations.** Physical places where shifts happen - rooms, entrances, stages, the kitchen. Each location can host different types of shifts.

**Angel Types.** The different roles angels can fill - general helpers, bar staff, video crew, security. Each type has its own requirements and restrictions.

**Shift Types.** Categories of work that define what kind of help is needed. A shift type might be "Door Guard" or "Camera Operator."

**Shifts.** Specific time slots at specific locations needing specific angel types. This is what angels actually sign up for.

As a bureaucrat, you typically can't create or modify these structures, but understanding them helps you answer questions and troubleshoot issues.

## FAQ Management

One area where bureaucrats often have full control is the FAQ. A good FAQ reduces the number of questions you receive and helps angels help themselves.

### When to Add FAQ Entries

If you notice the same question appearing repeatedly, it belongs in the FAQ. Common topics include:

- How to sign up for restricted angel types
- Where to find introduction meetings
- What to do if you can't make a shift
- How the goodie/t-shirt system works
- Contact methods and phone numbers

### Writing Good FAQ Entries

Keep answers concise and action-oriented. Angels are looking for quick answers, not comprehensive explanations. Link to other parts of the documentation when appropriate.

If a question requires different answers for different situations, consider splitting it into multiple entries rather than writing one complex answer.

## Questions System

Angels can submit questions through the system, and bureaucrats can answer them. This is different from FAQ - these are individual questions that may or may not become FAQ entries later.

When answering questions:

- Respond promptly when possible - angels may be blocked waiting for an answer
- If you don't know the answer, say so and point them to someone who might
- Consider whether the answer should become an FAQ entry

## Supporting Event Operations

Bureaucrats support the event's coordination work. During busy periods, you might be asked to help with:

- Processing arrival check-ins
- Answering basic questions from angels
- Looking up user information
- Making work log adjustments

Be ready to help where needed during events.

## Handling Special Situations

Sometimes situations arise that don't fit normal workflows:

**Last-minute changes.** If shifts need modification during the event, work with admins who have the necessary permissions.

**Emergency contacts.** Know how to reach event organizers and what issues should be escalated immediately.

**Data corrections.** If you find systematic errors in the data, document them and work with admins rather than trying to fix everything yourself.

{{% notice tip %}}
Document unusual situations as you handle them. Your notes help improve processes for future events and help train future bureaucrats.
